Integration Scope

Five integration components delivered across multiple Statement of Work phases, each with DocuSign sign-off before development started. All five are live in production.

01 Bidirectional CRM Sync Live

Companies and contacts created in Broadly automatically sync to Autotask PSA, and vice versa. Includes address, phone, email, and website field mapping with activity feed tracking and record source labels for every sync event.

02 SSO / OIDC Authentication Live

One-click sign-on from Autotask into Broadly. Configurable role-mapping layer lets each MSP define how their Autotask roles translate to Broadly permissions. Default: minimal access (secure).

03 Billing Reconciliation Live

Automated contract and invoice sync between Broadly billing and Autotask PSA contracts. Multi-currency support, prorated adjustments, and exception reporting for discrepancies.

04 AMP Product Configuration Live

Per-MSP product provisioning and white-label configuration through Kaseya's Powered Services Pro. Each MSP gets independent branding, product catalog, and feature flags.

05 Enterprise Escalation Workflow Live

SLA monitoring with severity-based routing and executive notification chains. Response-time tracking tied to contractual SLA tiers defined in the Statement of Work.
