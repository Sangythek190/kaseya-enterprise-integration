Integration Scope

Five major integration components delivered across multiple Statement of Work phases.

01 Bidirectional CRM Sync Live

Companies and contacts created in Vendasta automatically sync to Autotask PSA, and vice versa. Includes address, phone, email, and website field mapping with activity feed tracking for every sync event.

02 SSO / OIDC Authentication Live

Single sign-on using OpenID Connect for MSP technicians. Enables seamless authentication from Kaseya into Vendasta without separate credentials, with configurable role-based access control mapped from Autotask user roles.

03 Billing Reconciliation Live

Contract and invoice synchronization between Vendasta billing and Autotask PSA contracts. Handles multi-currency scenarios, prorated adjustments, and automated reconciliation with exception reporting.

04 AMP Product Configuration Live

Powered Services Pro AMP setup for MSP partners. Includes per-partner product provisioning, feature flag management, and white-label configuration to match each MSP's branding requirements.

05 Enterprise Escalation Workflow Live

Enterprise SLA monitoring with automated escalation tiers. Response-time tracking, severity-based routing, and executive notification chains integrated with the enterprise account management workflow.

Challenges & Decisions

Key technical and process decisions made during the integration.

Challenge

Enterprise SLA requirements — Kaseya expected contractual uptime and response-time guarantees exceeding standard platform commitments.

Decision

Built a dedicated escalation workflow with severity-based routing and executive notification chains. Defined SLA tiers in the Statement of Work with explicit penalty clauses.

Challenge

Multi-tenant MSP architecture — Each MSP operates as an independent business with its own clients, requiring strict data isolation.

Decision

Implemented per-MSP configuration isolation. Each Managed Service Provider gets an independent sync context with its own field mappings, billing rules, and access controls.

Challenge

Bidirectional sync conflicts — Concurrent edits in both Vendasta and Autotask could create data integrity issues.

Decision

Adopted a last-write-wins strategy with conflict logging. Built an exception dashboard for operations teams to review and manually resolve flagged discrepancies.

Challenge

SSO role mapping complexity — Autotask user roles did not map 1:1 to Vendasta permissions, risking over- or under-provisioning.

Decision

Created a configurable role-mapping layer allowing each MSP to define how Autotask roles translate to Vendasta access levels, with a secure default of minimal permissions.

Product Management Work

End-to-end ownership from discovery through delivery, coordinating across four teams and two time zones.

  • Requirements gathering with enterprise stakeholders across Kaseya product, engineering, and partner success teams over a multi-month discovery phase.
  • Statement of Work definition with DocuSign sign-off required before development started on each integration phase. Scope, timeline, and acceptance criteria documented per phase.
  • Cross-team coordination across Vendasta engineering (Chennai async team), enterprise sales, partner success, and Kaseya's technical integration team.
  • SLA definition and escalation design establishing response-time tiers, severity classifications, and executive escalation paths with Adam Stark as the enterprise account escalation point.
  • Acceptance criteria authoring covering happy path, edge cases, multi-tenant isolation, concurrent sync scenarios, SSO failure modes, and billing reconciliation validation.
  • Integration testing and verification — hands-on testing of bidirectional CRM sync, validating company and contact data flows between Vendasta and Autotask PSA in staging and production environments.
  • Mission Brief and Product Factory artifacts following the full chain: Vision Document, Mission Brief, Prototype, Request for Comments, Epic, and Stories.
